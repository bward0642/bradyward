01 About

I've spent the last few summers learning how money is actually managed — from the trust side at Truist to private wealth at Bryn Mawr Trust.

Most recently I worked as a Fiduciary, Investments, and Planning intern at Truist in Charlotte, auditing privately held and directed assets and reconciling lease files against system records. Before that I reconciled revenue reporting at Hershey Entertainment & Resorts and supported advisors serving high-net-worth families at Bryn Mawr Trust.

During the school year I'm a Financial Literacy Ambassador at Penn State, where I teach students about budgeting, student loans, and investing, and helped produce a podcast for communities across Pennsylvania. I'm also a member of the Sapphire Leadership Academic Program and am a Level I Candidate in the CFA® Program, sitting for the exam in February 2027.
